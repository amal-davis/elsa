About Elsa Chicken Curry Masala

Elsa Chicken Curry Masala is born from thirty-one years of getting one dish right. It starts with hand-picked Kerala peppercorns and whole curry leaves, dried slowly so none of the volatile oils escape, then stone-ground alongside a carefully balanced roster of coriander, turmeric, red chilli, cumin and cardamom.

Most commercial masalas are built around colour and shelf life. Ours is built around the moment you lift the lid. The aroma that fills the kitchen when the masala hits the hot oil is the smell of slow-dried whole spices, not spray-dried extract powder. That distinction is audible, visible and unmistakeable on the tongue.

How to use

  1. Heat oil in a heavy pan. Add sliced onions and cook until golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for two minutes.
  3. Add 1½ tsp of Elsa Chicken Curry Masala per 500 g of chicken.
  4. Stir for 30 seconds over medium heat until fragrant.
  5. Add tomatoes, cook down, then add the chicken and cook through.
  6. Finish with a sprig of fresh curry leaf and a squeeze of lime.

Ingredients

Coriander (34%), Red Chilli (18%), Black Pepper (12%), Turmeric (10%), Cumin (8%), Fennel (6%), Cardamom (4%), Curry Leaf Powder (4%), Cinnamon (2%), Cloves (1%), Bay Leaf (1%). No added salt. No MSG. No preservatives. No artificial colour.

ISO 22000:2018 Certified

Every batch is tested for microbiological safety, moisture content and colour consistency before it leaves our facility in Muttom, Kerala.
