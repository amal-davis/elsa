Our Story

About Elsa Curry Powder

The Heaven Valley Industries is founded with the noble mission of nurturing the health of individuals and thereby promoting social welfare and development. This ISO certified company has grown over the years as envisioned by its promoters.

V

Shri. Varghese Muttom

CEO & Co-Founder

"Nurturing the health of every family through the finest spices of Kerala."

With enormous investment and quality assurance, the firm has acquired an annual turnover of more than 10 lakhs. Its production per month is more than 1 tonne, catering to the needs of its regular and satisfied customers. The company complies fully with statutory and regulatory requirements.

A wide range of traditional spices and blended spice powders are produced and supplied exclusively to customers who consider health as wealth. Provisions for online ordering and home delivery, both in India and abroad, are the unique features of this company.

Innovative technology, production methods and preservation systems ensure consistently high quality organic range powders without any artificial preservatives, colouring agents, flavours or taste makers. Spices are procured from the finest Indian plantations in Kerala.

High quality flavour-lock packaging is adopted which preserves the flavour and aroma of the spices even for long periods.
